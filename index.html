MCA Project Case Study

Problem

Cloud-integrated ERP environments generate large volumes of audit and activity data. Manual review can make it difficult to identify unusual patterns efficiently.

Proposed Approach

The project proposes an AI-assisted cyber defense workflow that collects authorized audit telemetry, normalizes events, derives contextual signals and prioritizes suspicious activity for human analyst review.

Illustrative Signals

  • Rare privileged actions.
  • Repeated denied requests.
  • Novel source context.
  • Unusual timing or activity patterns.
  • Contextual relationship between multiple fields.

Important Evidence Boundary

This is a prototype-level academic project. It does not claim production deployment, measured enterprise accuracy or automated real-world containment.
